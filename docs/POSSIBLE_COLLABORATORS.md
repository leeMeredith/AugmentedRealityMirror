# Possible collaborators

ARM is a technical prototype at the intersection of visual feedback, rehabilitation, and depth-camera software. The areas below identify expertise that could help assess it. They do not imply that any person or organization has expressed interest, endorsed ARM, or agreed to participate.

| Area of expertise | Questions it could help assess |
| --- | --- |
| Limb-loss rehabilitation and physical or occupational therapy | Whether the visual experience fits a meaningful task or clinical workflow, and what adjustments would make a demonstration usable |
| Pain and motor-imagery research | Which questions about visual feedback are worth studying and which outcomes or comparisons would be appropriate |
| Rehabilitation engineering and computer vision | Sensor reliability, mirror alignment, display delay, calibration, and alternatives to the original Kinect |
| Human factors and accessibility | Comfort, controls, setup burden, and whether the interface can be used consistently |
| People with lived experience of limb loss | Whether the prototype addresses a relevant need and how its use feels in practice |
| Clinical research and data governance | Whether any proposed evaluation needs formal review and how participant information should be handled |

The immediate technical work is to confirm that the current JSON build runs with the Kinect, then document its mirror alignment, recording behavior, and limits using synthetic data. Any future evaluation would need its own scope and the appropriate clinical and institutional review. This repository does not establish a study or a collaboration.
