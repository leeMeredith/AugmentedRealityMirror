Augmented Reality Mirror

Getting started

This browser version offers a camera mirror, fullscreen and independent quadrant adjustments. You can save and reload your view and camera preference. Pain entries are a later milestone.

Start with a comfortable view

  1. Place your computer or camera on a stable surface, with enough light to see yourself. Adjust the physical camera to include the area you want to view.
  2. Choose Start camera and allow the browser to use the camera. Microphone access is not requested.
  3. If the wrong camera opens, choose another in the Camera list and press Use selected camera.
  4. Raise a hand to check the starting view. If you need the opposite direction, press Reverse view. Pressing it again returns to the previous direction. A loaded setup restores its saved choice.
  5. Choose Stop when finished. Closing or leaving this page also releases this page's camera stream.

Use a larger view

Choose Fullscreen in the top toolbar. It works on your current screen; a second monitor is not required. The settings panel hides so the image has more room. Use Show controls to adjust your view, and Hide controls when finished. On a narrow screen the controls open over part of the image.

The toolbar stays visible, including Stop camera. Press Escape or the exit button to return to the normal page. Leaving fullscreen does not stop the camera or change your orientation or setup. The previous panel visibility is restored.

If your browser cannot enter fullscreen, the view expands inside the browser window instead. Use Exit expanded view or Escape to return. The whole camera frame is fitted without stretching or cropping, so borders may remain. Fullscreen does not increase camera resolution or reveal more of the scene.

Fullscreen always starts with a button press; loading a setup will not enter it automatically.

Adjust one quadrant

Start the camera, choose Fullscreen, then Show controls. Scroll the settings panel to Quadrants. The four buttons refer to positions on the screen. Select the receiving quadrant you want to change.

  • Left/right mirror copies and reflects the opposite side into the selected quadrant.
  • Top/bottom mirror copies and reflects the opposite row. Both toggles on use the diagonal quadrant; both off show the normal image.
  • Horizontal position and Vertical position move the image within the selected quadrant. The dividing lines stay fixed. Uncovered edges stay dark, and the image cannot cover another quadrant.
  • Reset quadrant turns off both toggles and centers both sliders for just that quadrant.

Each quadrant remembers its own adjustments when you select another. Slider values are percentages of quadrant width or height; positive values move right or down. You can also focus a slider and use the arrow keys. The camera section's Reverse view changes the whole starting image before these adjustments.

All sources come from the original camera image, so editing one quadrant never changes another quadrant's source. Check your posture and physical camera position each time; saved settings cannot move the camera for you.

Save your setup for another day

A setup name is optional. If you enter one, it begins the downloaded filename. Choose Save setup, then keep the JSON file in a folder you can find again. Your browser may save it to Downloads or ask where to put it.

Next time, choose Load setup and select that file. This restores your mirror direction, all four quadrant settings and preferred camera name. It does not start or switch the camera automatically. Confirm the camera choice and press Start camera or Use selected camera.

Setups from earlier builds still load with their original direction and no quadrant adjustments. New quadrant setups need build 6 or later.

A setup cannot move the physical camera or restore your posture. Check your position each time. Camera names can change or be shared by two devices, so always check the preview.

Only setups saved by this browser version are supported here. Desktop ARM study-session files will be rejected with an explanation.

If the camera does not open

  • Permission denied: check the site's camera permission in your browser and the browser's permission in your computer's privacy settings. Then press Start camera again.
  • Camera busy: close other applications using the camera and try again.
  • Camera missing: check its connection, choose Default camera, and retry.
  • Restricted computer: an administrator may need to allow the site or camera. This app cannot bypass those restrictions.
  • Still waiting: look for a browser permission prompt. You can press Stop even while that prompt is open; a late permission response will not start the preview.
  • Changes do not appear: stop the camera and reload the page. The build label at the bottom helps identify which version is running. When checking startup orientation, start before loading a setup, since the setup restores its own flip choice.

Where information goes

The camera image is processed on this device. This version has no video recording, accounts, analytics, or upload service. Saving creates a local JSON download containing the setup name, camera preference, mirror choice, and save date. Loading reads the file on your device.

A hosting provider may log ordinary website requests, such as your IP address. The app does not send it camera images or setup files. A file saved to a cloud-synced folder may be synced by that folder's service.

About this version

ARM is an experimental visual-feedback tool. These instructions explain its controls; they are not a treatment plan or a promise of pain relief.
